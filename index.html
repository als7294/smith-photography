About the studio

Unique, memorable images since 1999.

Located in historic downtown Sayre, PA — always striving to be the area's premiere source for all types of photography.

Owner and photographer Jim Smith has been working in studios for nearly 30 years. The experience of shooting thousands of sessions, weddings and events has honed his style: classic yet contemporary, with a focus on subject and great lighting.

Never one to follow trends, our images and editing strive to be timeless — with an eye on the best of current looks and technique.
